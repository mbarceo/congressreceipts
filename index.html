How we check

Every claim has a receipt

Official records firstVotes come from the House Clerk and senate.gov, money from FEC filings, bills from govinfo, and words from members' own press releases and archived campaign sites.
Built, not typedVote tallies, percentages and dollar figures are computed from the raw records by scripts, so they can't be mistyped.
Checked against the sourceEvery quote, vote and figure is re-checked against its source. A misquote, a wrong vote or a wrong tally fails the check, and the data is fixed, never the checker.
Verdicts state their ruleWhere a page sums up a record ("Mostly no", "Mixed"), it says the rule that produced it, and shows every item behind it.

Donor names and addresses are never published, only employer and occupation. Each tracker's Sources page lists every claim with how and when it was checked.
