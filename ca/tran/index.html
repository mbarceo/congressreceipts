The district

CA-45 spans two counties

Tran represents cities in both Los Angeles County and Orange County. Prop 50 redrew the lines for the 2026 election. The new map takes effect Jan 3, 2027; until then he represents the 2023 lines.

Shaded areas are the parts of each city inside the district; the dark line is the district boundary. Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb (119th and 120th Congressional Districts). Ties to House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries

  • Voted for Jeffries for Speaker when the 119th Congress opened (Jan 3, 2025), and was one of five Democrats who formally nominated him for Speaker in the caucus vote on Nov 18, 2026. Roll call NBC News
  • Received $74,942 from Jeffries' committees: $56,942 through the Jeffries Battleground Protection Fund (2025–26), $10,000 from his leadership PAC (2024) and $8,000 from Jeffries for Congress (both cycles), per FEC filings. Money tab

  • Pointed to Jeffries' proposal on Israel aid in his statement backing the Massie amendment (Jul 2026). Source
