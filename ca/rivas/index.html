The district

CA-29 is mostly the northeast San Fernando Valley

Nearly everyone in the district lives in the City of Los Angeles, in communities like Pacoima, Sylmar, Panorama City and Van Nuys; the rest live in the City of San Fernando. Prop 50 redrew the lines for the 2026 election. The new map takes effect Jan 3, 2027; until then she represents the 2023 lines.

Shaded areas are the parts of each community inside the district; the dark line is the district boundary.
