Sources and verification

How every claim is checked

Every sourced claim on this site is listed below with its source, how it was checked, and when. Votes, bills and money are checked against official government records; quotes are matched word for word against {{poss}} press releases or saved copies of the source pages.

Method

Five kinds of checks

Votes

Each vote is matched to {{voteSourceLong}}: how {{short}} voted, and every tally quoted.

Bills and committees

Sponsorships and public laws are matched to govinfo.gov's official bill records. Committee seats are read from {{poss}} own entry in the House Clerk's member file.Committee seats are matched to the senate.gov committee membership lists.

Money and vote math

Dollar figures, percentages and ranks are recomputed from FEC filings and roll-call files with separate code, then compared with the number shown.

{{Poss}} press releases

Quotes and dollar figures must appear in the full text of the release cited. Every release on the Statements page is saved for this.

Other sources

Each cited page is fetched and saved. Quotes, figures and required terms in the claim must appear on it. A source that can't be read automatically (such as a scanned PDF) is read by hand, and its row says what was read.

Primary sources are official records and the people's and groups' own sites: the House Clerksenate.gov, govinfo, the FEC, election officials, {{poss}} official and campaign websites, caucus rosters and party organizations. Secondary sources are news reports and trackers. Partial means the source loaded but some detail could not be matched; Failed means a vote, tally or quote did not match, or the source did not load.
