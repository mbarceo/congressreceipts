    The rules behind the numbers

    Only public statementsA senator counts for a candidate only if they said so in public, or put out a statement. A report that someone is "leaning" does not count.
    The candidatesSchumer and Van Hollen each have one vote, and each is counted for themselves. The totals show senators plus the candidate.
    Undecided or no statement"Undecided" means the senator said they haven't chosen. "No position stated" means the senator declined to say, or we found nothing on the race. Neither one is a guess about how they will vote.
    28 votesFifty-five Democratic senators can vote, so 28 is a majority. Some outlets count 29 of 56 because they include Klobuchar.
    ReceiptsEach name links to the best source we found. "Own statement" is the senator's office or post. "News report" is a published story. "Secondhand list" is a news outlet's list. "Older than the race" is from before the election.
