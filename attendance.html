Who’s showing up

Missed votes, town halls, and how each member voted on the critical bills their tracker follows. Every number is counted from the official roll-call records (House Clerk and senate.gov) and links to its source.

Attendance

Votes missed

"Missed" means recorded as Not Voting. Members miss votes for illness, family, travel or other official duties; this page reports the record, not the reason. The comparison counts members who served at least 80% of the Congress, and ties count as not fewer.

Critical votes

When they broke with their party or didn't vote

Each dot is a vote the member's tracker treats as critical (the votes behind its Stances page and evidence cards), so the House and Senate rows cover different bills. "Broke" means voting Yea or Nay against how most Democrats voted (in the Senate, the Democratic caucus, counting independents who caucus with them). Hover or tap a dot for the bill.

Town halls and public events

From each member's own press releases and local news, as collected on their tracker. Televised national town halls are listed but not counted as town halls with constituents.

Sources: House Clerk roll-call XML (clerk.house.gov/evs) and senate.gov roll-call XML, counted by scripts/build_attendance.py, which refuses to build if a tracker's vote list and the official files disagree.
